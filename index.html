About Me

I’m a junior at Johns Hopkins University pursuing a B.S. in computer science. I do research at the Normativity Lab under Gillian Hadfield, studying how AI agents learn social norms and reason about what people expect of them.

At the Alliance AI Lab, I apply this research to human–robot interaction. I’m also an external collaborator with the Social AI Research Group, where I’ve contributed to Sorrel, an open-source framework for building multi-agent environments.

I’m broadly interested in AI alignment, especially what it would take for models to reason more like people. My current interests include mechanistic interpretability, neuroscience-inspired machine learning architectures, emergent cognition, and multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL).
